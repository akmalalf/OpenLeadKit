# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in OpenLeadKit a harassment-free experience for everyone,
regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and
expression, level of experience, education, socioeconomic status, nationality, personal
appearance, race, caste, color, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

We pledge to act and interact in ways that contribute to an open, welcoming, diverse, inclusive,
and healthy community.

## Our standards

Positive behavior includes empathy, respect for differing viewpoints, constructive feedback,
accepting responsibility, and focusing on what is best for the community. Unacceptable behavior
includes sexualized language, trolling, insulting comments, harassment, publishing private
information without permission, and other conduct inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Enforcement responsibilities

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing acceptable behavior and may
remove, edit, or reject contributions inconsistent with this Code of Conduct.

## Scope

This Code applies in all project spaces and when an individual officially represents the project.

## Enforcement

Report abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behavior through the repository's
[private GitHub reporting channel](https://github.com/akmalalf/OpenLeadKit/security/advisories/new)
and identify it as a Code of Conduct report. The maintainers will respect the reporter's privacy
and use the Contributor Covenant enforcement guidelines to determine a proportionate response,
ranging from correction and warning through temporary or permanent bans.
